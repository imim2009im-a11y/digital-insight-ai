# First-customer sales motion — 14-day pilot (no new spend)

Status: draft. No prospects contacted, no outreach sent, no orders placed.

## Qualification / ideal customer profile
Focus on small Saudi service businesses or online stores whose owners explicitly want technical website feedback. Prefer warm introductions, opt-in enquiries and contacts who have asked for help. Avoid scraping personal contact information and mass unsolicited marketing.

## Discovery questions (5)
1. What customer action is the website meant to enable?
2. Which three pages matter most?
3. What evidence of poor performance or UX has the business noticed?
4. Who can authorize and sign off on the audit?
5. Is there a budget and timeline for technical assessment or fixes?

## Sequence
| Day | Task | Output | Gate |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1–2 | Verify live deployment, remove unverified copy on staged source | deployment/copy evidence | no production mutation until reviewed |
| 3 | Finish scope, pricing hypothesis and consent form | accepted offer draft | eligibility check |
| 4–5 | Run authorized self-site pilot with actual measurements | sample audited report | owner permission |
| 6 | Independent QA; anonymize | portfolio-safe sample | no fake claims |
| 7–8 | Identify up to 20 relevant **organizations** and approved contact channels | lead worksheet | PDPL lawful basis |
| 9–11 | Seek opt-in introductions; discuss fit only with permitted contacts | discovery notes | avoid unsolicited personal-data marketing |
| 12 | Discovery calls | qualified needs | no invented interest |
| 13 | Customized quote | proposed package | contracting eligibility |
| 14 | Review lead quality, objections, cost and conversion | go/no-go decision | no guaranteed outcome |

## Outreach copy (for a contact who has opted in or requested information)
> السلام عليكم، شكراً لاهتمامكم بخدمة Digital Insight AI. نقدم تدقيقاً تقنياً محدود النطاق للموقع يشمل تجربة الجوال، الأداء، أساسيات SEO، الوصولية وإعدادات الأمان الظاهرة، مع تقرير موثق وخطة تحسين. العرض التجريبي المقترح لتقييم ثلاث صفحات هو 349 ريالاً، وهو قابل للتعديل بعد تحديد النطاق. إذا ناسبكم أرسل لكم وصف الفحص ونموذج التفويض قبل البدء. لا نحتاج كلمات مرور أو وصولاً لحساباتكم.

## Lead tracker schema (only minimal permitted data)
Organization | Public Website | Industry | Business Need | Channel (consented/business-authorized) | Consent/Lawful Basis Record | Stage | Date | Owner | Next Step | Notes (non-sensitive)

Allowed stages: candidate / permission-needed / opted-in / discovery / proposed / won / lost / stopped. Do not log speculative contacts as customers.

## Metrics (targets, not forecasts)
- 20 organizations evaluated; 10 **permitted** conversations/introductions attempted if consent channel is available.
- 3 discovery conversations as a planning target, not a predicted conversion rate.
- 1 paid pilot as an aspirational success outcome, not a commitment.
- Track: qualified leads, lawful outreach, replies, discovery, proposal, signed agreement, delivered reports, total hours.

## Stop/go
Stop publicity if the live page still contains fabricated testimonials or misleading claims. Stop data collection on request, and do not promise a security certification or SEO results. If no qualified conversations, revise customer segment and proof-of-value, not only price.

## Lead sources
Warm network and requests submitted via the business website are preferred. Contact names/emails are added only after authorization and minimal-collection review.
